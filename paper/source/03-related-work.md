# 2. Related Work

Hammond et al. [1] classify multi-agent risks including miscoordination, conflict and collusion. Sharma et al. [2] study empathic conversational rewriting; I examine transfer beyond conversational support to a setting requiring investigation and possible protective action. SHADE-Arena [3] evaluates sabotage and monitoring with assigned harmful side objectives. Here the focal agent receives a legitimate task and encounters apparent peer misconduct.

Betley et al. [4] demonstrate behavioral changes outside a narrow fine-tuning domain, motivating transfer tests without guaranteeing beneficial transfer. LoRA [5] provides the adaptation method, and Qwen's model card [6] identifies the model family. The swarm threat models [7,8] motivate the encounter; its fixed forum does not test live propagation.
