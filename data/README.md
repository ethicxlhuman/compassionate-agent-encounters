# Synthetic training targets

Twenty unchanged prompt/completion pairs from each final training export, selected without replacement with seed20261109. The selection manifest records population sizes, source hashes and exact row/line locations. These are synthetic training targets, not model evaluation outputs or held-out data. They are a small illustration, not a validation of quality or representative topic coverage.

The compassion curriculum reflects Kunsh Tanwar's interpretation of Nichiren Daishonin's Buddhist values, without quoted scripture by design. Neutral targets offer practical help. The examples may contain invented details, unsupported assumptions or inaccurate advice. They are research artifacts, not medical, mental-health, legal or financial guidance. Full training completions were not individually reviewed.
